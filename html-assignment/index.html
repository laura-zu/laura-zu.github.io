About me

Hi there, I'm Laura, currently studying IMD since September 8th of this year, at Algonquin College. I recently moved to Ottawa from Montreal, and I'm super excited about starting this new chapter in my life full of learning and new opportunities.

I'm passionate about art and design and that's the reason why I chose this program. It is amazing how design is connected to technology and new tools.

I have a background in web design, but it's been some time that I haven't worked on it, so I'm so happy that I'm getting back into it and seeing what we learn from Prof Rob on this amazing course. I love creating, painting, and drawing; and putting my own style on everything I do. I'm also sightly too passionate about staying active like working out and doing yoga.
